# User Persona - Beginner Programmer

---

## Jane Doe

**Age:** 25
**Role:** Student/Junior software engineer (limited technical experience; some study)
**Tech background:** Comfortable with everyday workplace software and has dabbled in basic coding (e.g. scripting, query languages). Now learning Python to support her new work requirements, and keep pace with colleagues.

> *"I get the basics, but I keep hitting walls – and I don't always know what I don't know."*

---

## Profile

Jane has picked up some coding through a mix of online courses and ad-hoc tutorials. She can read simple code and tweak existing scripts, but writing something new from scratch in Python feels slow and uncertain – in particular as she hasn’t learned it before. She turns to generative AI tools when she gets stuck, but often accepts the output without fully understanding it.  This creates problems when something  breaks, as she doesn’t know where to start fixing it. She is motivated, but time-poor, and wants to optimise her skills my learning – not just copying and pasting code.

---

## Needs

- To build genuine understanding of concepts, not just working snippets
- To learn in a low-friction setting that keeps her engaged, even when things are tought
- A tool that meets her at her level without being condescending or overly basic
- Confidence that the help she's getting is correct
- The ability to tell when the tool is not correct

## Wants

- Clear, plain-language explanations of why code works, not just what it does (or what she has to do)
- Quick answers when she's stuck, without having to wade through long tutorials or read swathes of generated code
- A learning experience that feels collaborative rather than transactional
- To feel supported and not overwhelmed

## Pain Points

- Knowledge gaps she can't easily articulate 
- Generative AI tools that produce seemingly polished code, but she often doesn’t know where to start debugging, and cannot explain why it works
- Tutorials and documentation are not pitched at her specific level (either too hard or too easy)
